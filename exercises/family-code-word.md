title: Family code word

---

**Time:** 5 minutes, or homework. **Goal:** each person plans to choose a code word with their family, in person, this week. Emergency scams rely on panic and a believable voice. A scammer can't answer "What's our word?"

## Choosing a good word

- **Easy to remember under stress.** A silly phrase or family joke.
- **Not findable online.** No pet names, streets, or schools.
- **Shared in person**, or on a call you started. Never by text, email, or social media.
- **Known to everyone**, including grandparents and kids old enough to answer the phone.

## The rule

If someone calls in an emergency and asks for money, help, or secrecy, ask for the word. If they can't give it, hang up and call the person on the number you already have. If they give it but something feels wrong, call back anyway.

## In the session

Ask people to write on their [takeaway](../handouts/one-page-takeaway.md) who they'll set a word with and when. Nobody says a word out loud.
