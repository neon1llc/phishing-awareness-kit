---
title: Invoice and bank change fraud
description: "An email from a supplier, contractor, or client, sometimes from their real hacked account, asking you to pay to a new bank account."
---

## What it looks like

"Please note we've changed banks. Use the attached details for this invoice and all future payments." It may come from a supplier's hacked account, or an address one letter off, when a payment is due. The same trick hits home buyers ("updated wiring instructions") and payroll ("send my direct deposit to my new account").

## The tell

**Any change to where money goes, arriving by email.** Add hurry ("settle today") and it's almost certainly fraud.

## What to do

Call the supplier on a number already on file, not one in the email. No exceptions, including the boss and the biggest customer. For a home purchase, call your title company or lawyer on a number from your original paperwork before wiring.

## If it already happened

Ask your bank to recall the wire, then report it at [ic3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov/). Tell the real supplier their account may be hacked.

Full card: [Never change payment details on the strength of an email](https://github.com/neon1llc/ready-secure-go/blob/main/content/practices/scams-and-social-engineering/scam-payment-change-verification.md).
