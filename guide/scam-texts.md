---
title: Package, toll, and account texts
description: "Text messages about a held package, an unpaid toll, or a problem with an account, with a link to fix it."
---

## What it looks like

- "Your package is on hold. Update your address: [link]"
- "Unpaid toll balance of $4.15. Pay today to avoid a $50 fee: [link]"
- "Your account has been suspended. Verify now: [link]"
- "Is this you in this video?" or "Hi, is this Sarah?" from a stranger.

## The tell

- A link you didn't ask for, to a site you didn't choose.
- A small amount of money. Scammers want your card details, not the $4.
- An odd sender: a long number, an email address, or a foreign number.
- A "wrong number" text that turns into a friendly chat often starts a [romance or investment scam](romance-investment.md).

## What to do

Don't tap or reply. USPS texts only people who signed up for tracking, and never with links. Check tracking in the seller's app, or open your toll account the usual way. Forward scam texts to **7726** (SPAM), then delete them.

## If it already happened

If you entered card details, call your card company. If you entered a password, change it and turn on two-step sign-in.
