title: Tech support pop-ups and calls
description: "A screen warning that your computer is infected, or a call from 'Microsoft' or 'Apple,' leading to remote access and payment."
---

## What it looks like

A browser warning: "Your computer is infected! Do not close this window. Call Microsoft Support at [number]." Or a caller from a "tech company" who found a problem on your computer. The "technician" has you install a remote access app, shows scary screens, then asks for payment, often gift cards or a "refund" of money they claim they overpaid.

## The tell

- Real tech companies don't put phone numbers in pop-ups or call out of the blue.
- They want **remote access** to your computer.
- They want payment by gift card, wire, crypto, or a "refund."

## What to do

Don't call the number. Close the browser. If it won't close, force quit (Ctrl+Alt+Delete on Windows, Command+Option+Escape on a Mac) or restart. If someone calls, hang up.

## If it already happened

Disconnect the computer from the internet. Uninstall the remote access app, or have someone you trust check the computer. Change passwords from a different device, starting with email and banking. Call your bank if you paid or let them see your account.
