---
title: Job and task scams
description: "Job offers that pay for simple online tasks, ask you to deposit a check and send some back, or charge fees for training or equipment."
---

## What it looks like

- A text: "We saw your resume. Earn $300 a day liking videos from home."
- Simple tasks pay at first, then you deposit your own money (often crypto) to "unlock" bigger ones.
- A check arrives for equipment from "our vendor," and you send back the rest. The check bounces days later.
- A chat-only interview, then a request for your Social Security number and bank details before any contract.

## The tell

- You didn't apply, or the pay is far too good for the work.
- **You pay them**: for training, equipment, or to unlock earnings.
- A check to deposit and forward, or a request to receive and reship packages.

## What to do

Look up the company yourself and use its official website. Real employers don't ask you to pay to work or to send money back from a check. Don't share your Social Security number until you've confirmed the job and signed an offer.

## If it already happened

Stop sending money. Tell your bank about any check you deposited. If you shared your Social Security number, go to [IdentityTheft.gov](https://www.identitytheft.gov/) and freeze your credit.
