---
title: Phishing emails
description: "Emails that pretend to be a company you use, to get your password, payment details, or a click on a harmful link."
---

## What it looks like

An email that seems to come from a bank, streaming service, shipping company, or your employer. It says your account is locked, a payment failed, or a document is waiting. A button takes you to a page that looks like the real sign-in page.

## The tell

- It wants you to sign in or pay **from a link in the email**.
- The sender's address, or the address the link goes to, doesn't match the company's real domain. Look closely: `paypa1-secure.example.com` isn't PayPal.
- Fear and hurry: "suspended within 24 hours."
- An attachment you weren't expecting, especially one that asks you to "enable content."

Polished emails with perfect spelling can still be fake.

## What to do

Don't click. Open the company's app, or type its address yourself, and check your account there. At work, use the report button or forward it to whoever handles security.

## If it already happened

If you entered a password, change it on the real site and anywhere else you used it, and turn on two-step sign-in. If you entered card details, call your card company. If you opened an attachment at work, tell IT now.
