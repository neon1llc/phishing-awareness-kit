---
title: Phishing emails
description: "Emails that pretend to be a company you use, to get your password, payment details, or a click on a harmful link."
---

## What it looks like

An email from a "bank," streaming service, shipper, or employer. Your account is locked, a payment failed, or a document is waiting. A button leads to a page that looks like the real sign-in.

## The tell

- It wants you to sign in or pay **from a link in the email**.
- The sender's or link's address doesn't match the company's real domain. Look closely: `paypa1-secure.example.com` isn't PayPal.
- Fear and hurry: "suspended within 24 hours."
- An attachment you weren't expecting, especially one that asks you to "enable content."
- A QR code in an unexpected email or text, or stuck over another on a sign. Check the address it opens before entering anything.

Perfect spelling doesn't make it real.

## What to do

Don't click. Open the company's app or type its address yourself. At work, use the report button or forward it to whoever handles security.

## If it already happened

If you entered a password, change it on the real site and anywhere else you used it, and turn on two-step sign-in. If you entered card details, call your card company. If you opened an attachment at work, tell IT now.
