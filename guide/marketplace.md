---
title: Online marketplace and overpayment scams
description: "Fake buyers and sellers on marketplace apps: overpayments, fake payment emails, verification codes, and items that never arrive."
---

## What it looks like

**Selling:** a buyer "accidentally" overpays and asks for the difference back, then the original payment bounces. Or a fake payment-service email says the money is waiting. Or they ask for a code "to prove you're real," which really sets up an account in your name.

**Buying:** a great deal, a seller who wants "friends and family" payment or a gift card, and an item that never comes. Rentals work the same way: a deposit for an apartment that isn't theirs.

## The tell

- Overpayments, refunds, or "pay the shipping and I'll reimburse you."
- Moving off the platform to text or email.
- Payment methods with no buyer protection.
- Requests for a verification code.

## What to do

Keep the conversation and payment inside the platform. Meet in a public place, ideally one the police suggest, and check items before paying. Use payment methods with buyer protection. For rentals, see the place in person and confirm who owns it.

## If it already happened

Report the user to the platform, and contact your payment app or bank right away.
