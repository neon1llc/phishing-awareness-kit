---
title: Fake fraud departments and code theft
description: "A caller claiming to be your bank's fraud team, or a tech company, who asks you to read back a sign-in or verification code."
---

## What it looks like

"This is the fraud department at your bank. We've blocked a suspicious charge. To verify you, I'm sending a code to your phone. Please read it back." Caller ID may show the bank's real name, because it's easy to fake. Also: "Apple support" about your iCloud, or "your carrier" about a SIM change.

## The tell

**They ask for a code you received.** That code is the second key to your account. The caller has your password or is resetting it, and needs the code to finish. A real fraud team never does.

## What to do

Hang up. Call the number on the back of your card, or open the company's app. Never read a code to anyone.

## If it already happened

Call the bank or company on a number you looked up. Change the account's password and check its recovery email and phone. If it was your phone carrier, ask for a PIN or port-out lock.
