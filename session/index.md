**Length:** 60 minutes (45 below). **Group size:** 5 to 40. See [audiences](../audiences/index.md) to adapt.

## Objectives

Everyone can:

1. Name the four pressures (hurry, fear, secrecy, and an unusual way to pay), and know that some scams build trust slowly instead.
2. Use the pause: hang up, look up, call back.
3. Show that two-step sign-in is on for their email.
4. Say who they'd tell, at home or at work, and who to call if money is lost.

## Materials

- The [slides](slides.md), turned into a presentation (see the [README](../README.md#the-slides)).
- One printed [takeaway](../handouts/one-page-takeaway.md) and [who-to-call card](../handouts/who-to-call.md) each.
- One set of [spot the scam](../exercises/spot-the-scam.md) cards per table.
- The [quiz](../handouts/quiz.md) for a before-and-after measure, and the [feedback form](../handouts/feedback-form.md).
- Wi-Fi details for the lab, on a slide or card.

## Run sheet

| Time | Segment | Slides | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Welcome and the hook | 1 to 3 | Show of hands: who's had a scam call or text this month? |
| 0:05 | The four pressures | 4 to 6 | The idea the rest builds on |
| 0:12 | The scams people see most | 7 to 11 | Five quick stories. Pick the ones your group will meet |
| 0:22 | Spot the scam | 12 | Tables sort the cards, [exercise](../exercises/spot-the-scam.md) |
| 0:32 | The pause, and a role-play | 13 to 14 | [Role-plays](../exercises/role-plays.md) |
| 0:40 | Lock the front door | 15 to 16 | Passwords, password managers, two-step sign-in |
| 0:45 | Two-step sign-in lab | 17 | Everyone turns it on for email, [lab](../exercises/two-step-lab.md) |
| 0:55 | Who to tell, and the takeaway | 18 to 20 | Code word, reporting, who to call, takeaway |

## The 45-minute version

Drop slides 9 to 11 (two scam stories instead of five), run the role-play with one pair in front of the room, and give the lab ten minutes.

## Setting up the room

Use tables of four to six, not rows, so the cards get discussed. For the lab, invite people to bring their phones, and see the [lab guide](../exercises/two-step-lab.md) for helpers.

## After the session

Send the takeaway as a file, with a link to the [field guide](../guide/index.md). If you ran the quiz, compare the scores and share them with the group's organizer.
