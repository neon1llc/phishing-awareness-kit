# If you think you've been scammed

**Act fast. A quick report gives the best chance of stopping the money.**

1. **Call your bank or card company first**, using the number on the back of your card. Ask them to stop or reverse the payment.
2. **Then use the right door for how you paid** (below).
3. **Change passwords** for any account the scammer may have seen, starting with email. Turn on two-step sign-in.
4. **Report it** (below). Reports help stop the same people reaching others.

## If you paid

| You paid by | Contact right away |
|---|---|
| Credit or debit card | The card issuer, on the number on the back of the card. Ask for a refund. |
| Gift card | The company that issued the card, on the number on the card. Say a scammer tricked you. Keep the card and receipt. |
| Wire transfer | The wire company and your bank. Ask for a recall. |
| Zelle or a bank transfer | Your bank or credit union. Ask them to reverse it. |
| Payment app (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal) | The app. Ask them to reverse it. |
| Cryptocurrency | The exchange or crypto ATM operator. Recovery is hard, so move fast. |
| Cash sent by mail | U.S. Postal Inspection Service: **1-877-876-2455**. |

Refunds are not guaranteed. The sooner you call, the better the chance.

## Where to report (United States)

| What happened | Where |
|---|---|
| Any scam or fraud | Federal Trade Commission: **ReportFraud.ftc.gov** |
| Someone used your identity, opened accounts, or filed taxes as you | **IdentityTheft.gov**, which gives you a recovery plan |
| Online fraud, business email compromise, or investment and crypto scams | FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center: **ic3.gov**. File even for a small loss, and include the full payment details. |
| Fraud against an older adult | National Elder Fraud Hotline: **1-833-FRAUD-11** (1-833-372-8311), weekdays 10am to 6pm Eastern time |
| A scam text | Forward it to **7726** (SPAM), then delete it |
| A threat or immediate danger | Local police, or **911** |

## Protect your credit

If personal details were shared, place a credit freeze with all three bureaus: **Equifax**, **Experian**, and **TransUnion**. It stops new credit being opened in your name. It's free to place and lift, and it doesn't affect your score.

## People who can help

- AARP Fraud Watch Network Helpline, free and toll-free: **877-908-3360**, weekdays 8am to 8pm Eastern time
- Someone you trust: a family member, friend, or the person who ran this session.
