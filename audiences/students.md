title: Students and young adults
description: "Adapting the session for high school and college students: job and marketplace scams, account takeovers, and where to get help."
---

## Pace and setup

- 45 to 50 minutes, to fit a class period.
- Students use their phones to check their own settings in the lab.
- For high school groups, invite the school counselor in advance, especially if you cover sextortion.

## Which scams to show

[Job and task scams](../guide/job-scams.md), [marketplace scams](../guide/marketplace.md), [scam texts](../guide/scam-texts.md), and [code theft](../guide/code-theft.md), framed around gaming and social media accounts ("I accidentally sent you my code, can you send it back?").

## What to emphasize

- **Your accounts are worth money.** Gaming, social media, and email accounts get stolen and sold. Two-step sign-in protects them.
- **Never send money or codes to get money.** Any job, prize, or deal where you pay first is a scam.
- **Sextortion.** Scammers pose as someone your age, trade photos, then threaten to share them unless paid. Say clearly: it's not your fault. Get help before deciding anything about paying, because paying rarely stops it. Block the account, keep the messages, and tell a trusted adult. [Take It Down](https://takeitdown.ncmec.org/), from the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, can help remove images of minors. Keep this part calm and factual.
- **Telling someone isn't snitching.** Name the people students can go to.

## Lab twist

Have students also check their most-used app: two-step sign-in, active sessions, and linked devices.

## Sources

- [Job scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams), from the FTC: pay-to-work offers, fake checks, and what to do if you paid.
- [Sextortion](https://www.missingkids.org/sextortion), from the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children: get help before paying, keep the messages, block, and report.
- [Take It Down](https://takeitdown.ncmec.org/), from NCMEC: help removing nude or explicit images of minors from participating platforms.
